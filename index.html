About Rehoboth

A peaceful retreat designed for business travelers, vacationing families, and special events.

Located in the scenic Lweza area along the Kampala-Entebbe Highway, Rehoboth Hotel & Apartments offers a blend of modern luxury and warm Ugandan hospitality. Our establishment features a range of accommodations from cozy single rooms to spacious two-bedroom apartments, all designed with your comfort in mind.

Beyond accommodations, we offer world-class amenities including a swimming pool, restaurant and bar, golf course, and versatile event spaces perfect for any occasion. Whether you're here for business or leisure, we promise an unforgettable experience.
